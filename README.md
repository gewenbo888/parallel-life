# Parallel Life · 平行人生

Parallel Life simulates the version of you born into another era and another place — career, relationships, status mobility, emotional weather, and the death scenario most likely to find you there. Bilingual (EN / 中文), dark/light, custom brand (Spectral display + Karla body + JetBrains Mono on archive `#0a0a14` with parallel-blue `#5b8df0` plus era-amber, archive-rose, mortality-violet, status-gold, fate-emerald accent codes). Hero centerpiece: animated multi-branch timeline SVG with five timeline nodes and randomised parallel branches. Four modules: P1 Five Life Dimensions (career, relationships, status, emotional weather, death scenario — the last two explicitly defended as the ones popular alternate-history skips), P2 The Simulator (5 eras × 8 places × 4 endowments = 160 templated scenarios, output is a five-dimension biography with era-correct mortality), P3 Counterfactual Engine (5 multi-paragraph canned probes — born-elsewhere, mortality-field defense, what-stays-across-lives, best-era-honestly, identity-across-lives — plus free-text heuristic that detects era/place anchoring and counterfactual register), P4 Guardrails (explicit refusal of utopian determinism, nationalist romance, "the past was better" framing, identity flattening, infinite-AI-generation slop).
